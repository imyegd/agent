\pi}{3}\right) I _ {c} \\ I _ {\beta} = \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) I _ {b} - \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) I _ {c} i \end{array} \right. \tag {11}
$$

最后化简得到

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} I _ {a} = I _ {a} \\ I _ {\beta} = \left(I _ {a} + 2 I _ {b}\right) / \sqrt {3} \end{array} \right. \tag {12}
$$
