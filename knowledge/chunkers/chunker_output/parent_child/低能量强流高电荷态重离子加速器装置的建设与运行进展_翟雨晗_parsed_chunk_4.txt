Abstract：    The  Low  Energy  High  Intensity  High  Charge  State  Heavy  Ion  Accelerator  Facility  (LEAF)  is  a national scientific instrument developed by the Institute of Modern Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, to provide high-current,  high-charge-state,  full-spectrum  low-energy  heavy  ion  beams  for  interdisciplinary  studies.  To  meet research  needs  in  nuclear  astrophysics,  atomic  and  molecular  physics,  and  nuclear  mater
