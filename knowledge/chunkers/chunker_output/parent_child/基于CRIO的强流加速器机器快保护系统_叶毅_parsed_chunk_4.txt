A machine-protection system is presented, which is built around a Compact Reconfigurable Input/Output(CRIO) controller that continuously monitors Marx discharges and magnet-power-supply loading to provide fast machine protection. It simultaneously communicate with the accelerator's central control system, which is based on the Experimental Physics and Industrial Control System(EPICS) architecture.
