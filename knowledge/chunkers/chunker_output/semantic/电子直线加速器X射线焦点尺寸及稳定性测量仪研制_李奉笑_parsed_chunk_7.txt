Finally, the complete system was experimentally tested on various electron linear accelerators including 2 MeV, 6 MeV, and 9 MeV systems, with comparative validation against the traditional "sandwich" method. [Results] The experimental results demonstrate that the measurement errors are within $1 0 \%$ compared to the "sandwich" method, with $X .$ -direction focal spot size of $1 . 9 5 ~ \mathrm { m m }$ versus $1 . 9 4 1 ~ \mathrm { m m }$ by the traditional method $0 . 5 \%$ error) and Y-direction size of $2 . 2 6 \ \mathrm { m m }$ versus 2.139 mm $5 . 6 \%$ error). The instrument enables rapid and repetitive measurements with excellent repeatability and successfully monitors focal spot position variations within $0 . 1 \ \mathrm { m m }$ during stability assessments. Individual instances show focal spot displacement reaching up to $0 . 6 \ \mathrm { m m }$ , indicating the instrument's capability to detect focal spot instabilities. [Conclusions] The developed measurement instrument in this study provides a fast, repeatable, and accurate method for X-ray focal spot size determination, not only quantifies focal spot dimensions but also enables dynamic monitoring of positional changes, offering significant advantages over traditional methods for enhancing NDT imaging quality assessment.
