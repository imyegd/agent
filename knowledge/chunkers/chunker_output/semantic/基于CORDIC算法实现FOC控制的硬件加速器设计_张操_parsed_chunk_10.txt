$$
\theta = \sum_ {i = 0} ^ {\infty} \theta_ {i} \tag {4}
$$

然后把坐标计算进行分解，那么第 $i + 1$ 次的坐标计算公式为

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x _ {i + 1} = x _ {i} - y _ {i} \tan \theta_ {i} \\ y _ {i + 1} = y _ {i} + x _ {i} \tan \theta_ {i} \end{array} \right. \tag {5}
$$
