Abstract [Background] The dimensions and positional stability of X-ray focal spots produced by electron linear accelerators significantly influence the quality of non-destructive testing (NDT) imaging and are critical determinants of spatial resolution in radiographic imaging. The conventional "sandwich" method recommended by International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) 62976: 2021 standards involves complex procedures and cannot achieve rapid and repetitive measurement of focal spot positional stability. [Purpose] This study aims to develop a slit translation scanning method and a dedicated measurement instrument for determining the X-ray focal spot size and stability of electron linear accelerators.
