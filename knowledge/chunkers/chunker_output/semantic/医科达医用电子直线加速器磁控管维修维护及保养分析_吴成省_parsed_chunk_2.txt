# Maintenance and Maintenance Analysis of Magnetron in Elekta Medical Linear Accelerator

WU Cheng-sheng YUAN Ge LI Ding-yan HUANG Guang-sen CHEN Jie CHEN Shao-juan LIU Jia MAI Yan-hua JIANG Lei* Radiotherapy Room, Cancer Hospital, Affiliated Hospital of Guangdong Medical University (Guangdong Zhanjiang 524000)

Abstract: In order to further improve the level of radiation therapy technology, this article takes the medical electronic linear accelerator of Elekta as the object, analyzes the operating principle of the magnetron, and combines four common faults such as low output dose of the magnetron, magnetron voltage value exceeding the limit, magnetron aging, and equipment interlocking during use to analyze and summarize maintenance methods. Finally, three maintenance suggestions are proposed: regular maintenance of the cooling system of the equipment, real-time monitoring of the electron beam, and flexible control of temperature and humidity. In order to effectively optimize the performance of the magnetron and provide reference for the maintenance and upkeep of the medical electron linear accelerator in radiation therapy in the future
