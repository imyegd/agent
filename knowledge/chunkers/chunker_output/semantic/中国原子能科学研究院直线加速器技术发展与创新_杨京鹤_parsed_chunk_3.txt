Abstract: The linear accelerator is a key technology of nuclear science and technology applications. Since its establishment, China Institute of Atomic Energy (CIAE) has been committed to the research and development of linear accelerator technology, and has a history of more than 70 years of innovative development. It has made great achievements and historic contributions to the multi-disciplinary and multi-application fields at CIAE, which has led to the progress of basic research and application technology of nuclear science and technology in China. After a review of the development history of linear accelerator, this paper focuses on the scientific and technological innovation and application of linear accelerator in the last 10 years. This paper has sorted out the continuous innovative development of the electron linear accelerator and high-intensity proton linear accelerator of CIAE.
