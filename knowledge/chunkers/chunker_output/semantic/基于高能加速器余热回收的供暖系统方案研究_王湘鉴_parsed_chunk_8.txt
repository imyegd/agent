The designed system achieves a high heating COP of 5.0. Economically, with a total initial investment estimated at 3.224 million RMB, the system generates annual operational cost savings of approximately 919,000 RMB compared to a conventional gas boiler baseline, leading to a short static payback period of 3.5 years － a period that could be further reduced under Beijing’s subsidy policies for renewable heating. In each heating season, the system is projected to save 260.5 tons of standard coal equivalent and reduce $\mathrm { C O } _ { 2 }$ emissions by 692.9 tons. Sensitivity analysis clearly identifies the operational load factor (directly affecting electricity consumption) and the electricity price as the two dominant economic variables, jointly accounting for over $9 5 \%$ of the variance in annual costs. A comparative summary against gas and electric boiler systems underscores the WHR system’s superior energy efficiency, lower operating cost, and significantly reduced carbon intensity. [Conclusions] In conclusion, this study successfully develops, designs, and validates a waste heat recovery heating system tailored for the BEPC. It confirms the technical feasibility and substantial economic and environmental benefits of utilizing stable, low-grade accelerator waste heat for building comfort heating without compromising scientific operations. The work provides a complete and systematic technical pathway － from resource assessment and technology selection to integrated design and multi-criteria evaluation － that can serve as a replicable blueprint for energy-saving retrofits in similar large-scale scientific infrastructures both in China and internationally. The findings offer a quantifiable, practical contribution to the green transformation of major research campuses and support the broader national goals of energy conservation, emission reduction, and sustainable development. Future work should focus on operational performance validation through long-term monitoring and investigate strategies such as thermal energy storage to further enhance system flexibility and annual utilization.
