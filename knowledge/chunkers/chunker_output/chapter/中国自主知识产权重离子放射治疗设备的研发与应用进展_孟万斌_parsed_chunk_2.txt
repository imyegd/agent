Research and Application Progress on Heavy Ion Radiotherapy Equipment with Independent Intellectual Property Rights in China

MENG Wanbin ， YE Yancheng ， LI Xiaojun ， MA Wenjun ， ZHU Fangfang

（ Gans u Wuwe i Cancer Hosp ital Wuwe i 733000 China ）

Abstract ： Heavy ion radiotherapy has emerged as a cutting-edge technology in global cancer treatment due to its precise dose distribution and potent tumor-cell killing capability. The development of heavy ion radiotherapy equipment with China ’ s independent intellectual property rights has involved decades of dedicated efforts. From the initial concept of “heavy ion cancer therapy ” proposed by the Institute of Modern Physics Chinese Academy of Sciences in 1993 to its clinical implementation at Gansu Wuwei Cancer Hospital in 2020 ， and further expansion by 2025 ， treatment centers have been established in multiple locations including Wuwei ， Lanzhou ， Hangzhou ， and Putian. Domestically developed systems have cumulatively treated over 2 000 pa - tients ， with clinical indications extended to more than 50 tumor types. This progress has broken the long-term foreign technological monopoly marking a transition from technological catch-up to partial leadership in the field. This paper reviews key milestones in the equipment ’ s development and clinical application ， systematically outlines critical stages from conceptualization to industri - alization and summarizes application scenarios and promotion outcomes across different phases. It aims to provide a reference for technological iteration and industrial planning in China ’ s high-end medical equipment sector.
