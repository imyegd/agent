Conclusion Medical cyclotrons

are characterized by high operating costs and often face a mismatch between single-center demand and installed capacity. Regional sharing of radiopharmaceuticals or coordinated supply across multiple PET/CT scanners can improve utilization. Policy-level dynamic pricing mechanisms that cover full life-cycle costs are recommended to ease cost pressures and support sustainable operations.
