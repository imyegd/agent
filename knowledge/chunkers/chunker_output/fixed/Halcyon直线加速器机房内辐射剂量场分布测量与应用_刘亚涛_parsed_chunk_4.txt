Conclusion Thermoluminescence dosimeter is suitable for multi-point in situ measurement of radiation field distribution in Halcyon accelerator treatment  rooms.  The  measurement  results  can  be  used  to  optimize  the  shielding  design  of  Halcyon  accelerator  treatment rooms.
