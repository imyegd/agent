A comprehensive evaluation model was then constructed, integrating thermodynamic performance (Coefficient of Performance, COP), full lifecycle economic analysis (including capital investment, operational savings, and payback period), and carbon emission reduction calculations. Finally, sensitivity analysis via tornado charts and Monte Carlo simulation was employed to identify and

quantify the key factors driving economic uncertainty. [Results] The results demonstrate a technically robust and economically attractive proposal. The designed system achieves a high heating COP of 5.0. Economically, with a total initial investment estimated at 3.224 million RMB, the system generates annual operational cost savings of approximately 919,000 RMB compared to a conventional gas boiler baseline, leading to a short static payback period of 3.5 years － a period that could be further reduced under Beijing’s subsidy policies for renewable heating.
