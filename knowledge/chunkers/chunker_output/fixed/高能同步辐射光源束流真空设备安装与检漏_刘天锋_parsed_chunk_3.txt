This paper systematically elaborates on the strategies and practices of vacuum leak detection for the HEPS storage ring vacuum system during the installation, commissioning, and operation phases. It focuses on expounding the leak detection scheme using the vacuum mode method of helium mass spectrometer leak detectors, detailing the procedures and solutions for locating various leak points (such as weld defects and improper installation of sealing rings), and analyzing the typical response characteristics of the instrument interface during leak detection.
