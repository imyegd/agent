Sensitivity analysis clearly identifies the operational load factor (directly affecting electricity consumption) and the electricity price as the two dominant economic variables, jointly accounting for over $9 5 \%$ of the variance in annual costs. A comparative summary against gas and electric boiler systems underscores the WHR system’s superior energy efficiency, lower operating cost, and significantly reduced carbon intensity. [Conclusions] In conclusion, this study successfully develops, designs, and validates a waste heat recovery heating system tailored for the BEPC. It confirms the technical feasibility and substantial economic and environmental benefits of utilizing stable, low-grade accelerator waste heat for building comfort heating without compromising scientific operations.
