Abstract The Institute of Earth Environment of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Xi'an Jiaotong University have jointly created an AMS center, under the support of Ministry of Science and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences and Ministry of Education. The Xi’an 3MV multi-element AMS system will be a national experimental platform for earth environmental studies, archeology, biomedical research and other fields related to the AMS technique.
