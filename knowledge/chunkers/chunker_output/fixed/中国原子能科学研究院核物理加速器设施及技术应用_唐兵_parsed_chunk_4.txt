CIAE has developed industrial projects such as small tandem accelerators, small neutron generators, accelerator mass spectrometer, and nuclear pore membrane production based on nuclear physics accelerator technology. In 2025, a $2\times 1.7\mathrm{MV}$ and a $2\times 3\mathrm{MV}$ tandem accelerators were delivered to users, and in 2026, a $2\times 6\mathrm{MV}$ tandem accelerator will be delivered, product standardization is preliminarily completed and technical self-reliance is achieved. CIAE has developed compact neutron generators and time-marking neutron generators with accuracy better than 1 ns and position-marking accuracy better than $0.3\mathrm{mm}$ . A high-flux neutron generator with capable of producing $3\times 10^{12}$ neutrons per second is under research.
