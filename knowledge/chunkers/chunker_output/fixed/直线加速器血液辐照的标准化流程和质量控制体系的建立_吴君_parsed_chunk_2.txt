Methods　With the use of the cone beam CT (CBCT) calibration water tank provided by the accelerator as the irradiation chamber, standard irradiation procedures and quality control measures were designed. The prescription dose was 25 Gy. Compared with traditional irradiation (solid water placed above and below the blood bag), the dose distribution, irradiation efficiency and quality control effect were evaluated. Results　In the standard irradiation, the blood bag received uniform dose, and the minimum dose reached 25.0 Gy, while the minimum dose in the traditional irradiation was 16.3 Gy. The emission time of standard irradiation was 4.57 minutes, slightly longer than the 4.10 minutes of traditional irradiation.
