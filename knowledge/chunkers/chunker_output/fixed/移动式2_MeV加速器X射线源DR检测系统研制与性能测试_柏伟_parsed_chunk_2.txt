Abstract: At present, the X-ray non-destructive testing (NDT) field urgently needs a digital NDT system based on mobile and miniaturized accelerator X-ray sources. In order to meet the urgent demand, a tracked vehicle mobile miniaturized and digital radiography (DR) NDT testing system prototype based on S-band accelerator X-ray sources is developed by using modular, standardized and compact design concepts and methods, and the prototype performance is experimental tested. The experimental results show that the energy of the accelerator X-ray source in the system exceeds $2.8\mathrm{MeV}$ , and the maximum dose rate at 1 meter in front of the target is $405\mathrm{mGy / min}$ .
