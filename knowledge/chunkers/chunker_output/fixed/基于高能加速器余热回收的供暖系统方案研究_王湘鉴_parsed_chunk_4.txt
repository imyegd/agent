While international examples such as CERN demonstrate the principle, applying waste heat recovery to existing, medium-scale accelerators with variable operational schedules poses distinct challenges. This study explicitly targets three core problems: (1) accurately quantifying the available waste heat potential and matching its intermittent profile with stable building heating demand; (2) designing a non-intrusive system integration scheme that guarantees zero interference with the accelerator’s primary scientific operations; and (3) conducting a holistic techno-economic-environmental assessment to verify the project’s viability and provide a replicable evaluation framework.
