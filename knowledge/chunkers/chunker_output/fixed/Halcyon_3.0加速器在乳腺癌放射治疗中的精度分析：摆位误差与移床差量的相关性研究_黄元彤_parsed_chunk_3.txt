Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) was used to verify and record setup errors in the vertical (VRT), longitudinal (LNG), and lateral (LAT) directions, as well as the total displacement (D). The couch shifts calculated by the treatment planning system (TPS) were recorded in the vertical (ΔVRT), longitudinal (ΔLNG), and lateral (ΔLAT) directions, with the total displacement (ΔD) calculated. Spearman correlation analysis was employed to evaluate the relationship between setup errors and couch shifts. Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves were plotted to assess the predictive performance of couch shifts for setup errors, using a threshold of 3mm to define significant deviations (positive for risk, negative for no risk). The area under the curve (AUC) was calculated. Results: Setup errors showed positive correlations with couch shifts across all directions.
