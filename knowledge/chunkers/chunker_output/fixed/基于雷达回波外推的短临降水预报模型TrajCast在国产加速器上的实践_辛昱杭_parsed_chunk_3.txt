[Context] Traditional numerical weather prediction has uncertainty in precipitation forecasting within the first two hours, and radar echo extrapolation methods based on physical models, such as optical flow and crosscorrelation methods, have limited prediction accuracy and stability under complex meteorological conditions. [Methods] In this study, we employ specialized datasets for short-term intense precipitation of an AI application developed by China Meteorological Administration. Based on TrajGRU, a deep learning network architecture, we establish the short-term and imminent precipitation forecasting model TrajCast.
