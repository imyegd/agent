TEVA resin，however，excels under acidic conditions. $1 \mathrm { m o l / L }$ nitric acid elutes most molybdate while perrhenate adsorbs onto the resin. Perrhenate recovery surges with $8 \mathrm { m o l / L }$ nitric acid elution，retaining less than $1 \%$ molybdenum. Alumina strongly adsorbs molybdate at $\mathrm { p H } \leqslant 7$ ，and normal saline effectively eluates perrhenate. A combined separation process leveraging both alumina and TEVA resin is proposed. Molybdate is adsorbed by alumina, which is followed by perrhenate elution with saline and further TEVA resin purification.
