A machine-protection system is presented, which is built around a Compact Reconfigurable Input/Output(CRIO) controller that continuously monitors Marx discharges and magnet-power-supply loading to provide fast machine protection. It simultaneously communicate with the accelerator's central control system, which is based on the Experimental Physics and Industrial Control System(EPICS) architecture. A Field Programmable Gate Array(FPGA) module inside the CRIO guarantees the response speed of the fast-protection logic, and a Real-Time(RT) module acts as an Input/ Output Controller(IOC) within the EPICS framework, subscribing to and publishing EPICS process variables to achieve seamless integration across heterogeneous platforms.
