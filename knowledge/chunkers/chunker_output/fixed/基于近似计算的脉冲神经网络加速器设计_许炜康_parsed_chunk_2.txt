However, in applications highly sensitive to power consumption, further reducing power consumption remains a crucial objective. Approximate computing simplifies design by introducing a certain degree of error, offering new opportunities for energy-efficient hardware design for fault-tolerant applications. This paper explores methods for applying approximate computing to SNN accelerators. Firstly, through analysis and experiments tailored to the application characteristics of SNNs, the distribution characteristics of input data for numerous adders in SNN accelerators are summarized. Based on these characteristics, an application-sensitive error evaluation metric for approximate arithmetic components, named AARE (application-aware approximation error), is proposed.
