Abstract Technetium- $. 9 9 \mathrm { m }$ （ $^ { 9 9 \mathrm { m } } \mathrm { T c }$ ），a crucial nuclear medicine，primarily stems from $9 9$ Mo decay in nuclear reactors. However，aging reactors have disrupted the 99Mo and 99mTc supply chain. Proton accelerators present a promising alternative for $^ { 9 9 } \mathrm { M o }$ and 99mTc production，yet they yield low-radioactivity 99Mo and face inefficient separation through alumina column. This study explores activated carbon，TEVA resin，and alumina for molybdate and pertechnetate separation.
